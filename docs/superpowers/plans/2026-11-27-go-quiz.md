# Go Series Quizzes Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Give all 20 posts of the `golang` series a review quiz in **quiz matrix v2**, `code` domain (tracks `interview` and `ai`). Ten foundation posts get 20 questions, ten deeper posts get 40: 600 questions, 100 sets.

**Branch:** `feat/go-quiz` (cut from `main` after PR #84).
**Quiz spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-quiz-matrix-v2.md`.
**Authoring brief (agents):** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-go-quiz-authoring-brief.md`.

## Global constraints

- Site tokens only (no hex), light + dark, bilingual `{en, vi}`, quizzes work without JS.
- Every Go snippet compiles and behaves as the question says, checked on Go 1.27.1. Output, panic, race, size and allocation questions carry `verifiedBy` with the real command and output.
- Figure `alt`, in-drawing labels and `aria-label` are neutral descriptions (the quiz page prints `alt` as a visible caption, `QuizMedia.astro:23`). Code questions use a `code` media block; figures only where something must be seen.
- No "illustrative" labels; an invented number states its assumption in the sentence. No real AI product names in `ai` questions.
- One agent owns one post's quiz and edits only its own files. No post text is edited; wrong spots in posts are reported and logged in `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Sizes: posts 1-10 of the series = 20 questions (4 sets); posts 11-20 = 40 questions (8 sets).
